Condoms are among the best ways to prevent the transmission of sexually transmitted infections and protect against unwanted pregnancy, but they often carry a stigma that can make some hesitant to use them. Whether people are afraid they’ll put on a condom incorrectly or feel too embarrassed to buy them, there are ways to overcome the feelings they may have concerning these prophylactics. If you’re less than comfortable with condoms, here are a few tips to help you get past your hang-ups.
